# Members, Attendance & Outreach

Keep the three core domains clearly separated:

**Members**
→ Member identity and profile information.

**Attendance**
→ Historical attendance records and participation data.

**Outreach**
→ Identifying and following up with inactive members.

Do not duplicate member information inside attendance or outreach records unnecessarily.

Use attendance history to determine inactivity rather than maintaining multiple independent sources of truth.

Keep outreach history as historical records rather than overwriting previous follow-ups.
